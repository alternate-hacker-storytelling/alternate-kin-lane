The light next to each bed appears to for each spa members, but it is also an early experience to see if we can generate electricity from each bed. We are running a test over the next six months to see how much voltage can be produced from just the night time sleeping period, but also day time naps. 

Think of the bed like one of those mobile phone charging mats where you lie the phone on it’s back and it gets charged. Each bed will allow spa members to recharge, but we will also be using their energy to recharge a small battery in the base of each light on the headboard, providing the data we need.

We don’t want to get ahead of ourselves, and this test is with just ten beds at our spa in China, but if this test is successful we will introduce new spas in the top 10 countries. Our goal is to get the spa bed pods in place out ahead of the advances in charging and energy extraction—eventually the energy will catch up to our rollout of pods

Our spa members will love that they can provide energy while recharging themselves. Right now the spa members pay for their membership, but eventually we believe we can offer a discount on their spa member depending on how much energy they produce, and who knows maybe someday spas can be free for everyone!
